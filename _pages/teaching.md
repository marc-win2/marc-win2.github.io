## Teaching and supervision

**Goethe University Frankfurt · 2019–2025**

- Taught tutorials and exercise classes across more than ten semesters in theoretical physics, including classical mechanics, electrodynamics, quantum mechanics, advanced quantum mechanics, quantum field theory, and statistical physics and thermodynamics.
- Served as lead tutor for three lectures. For a mandatory bachelor's course with more than 70 students, I coordinated up to ten tutors, designed exercise sheets, proofread lecture notes, gave substitute lectures, and managed student communication.
- Advised one B.Sc. thesis student, providing feedback on methodology, analysis, and thesis writing.
- Gave substitute lectures in _Mathematical Supplementaries to Theoretical Physics 2_ in 2022.

## Seminar coordination

I organized and hosted the joint specialist seminar _Special Topics in QCD at Finite Temperature and Density_ for four semesters. The seminar connected the local chiral field theory group with projects A03 and A06 of the DFG-funded research center [_CRC-TR 211_](https://crc-tr211.org/) and supported exchange and discussion across research groups.

At Deutsche Bundesbank, I contribute to seminar on the foundations of distributed consensus and blockchains. We present and discuss topics based on Elaine Shi's 2020 manuscript, [_Foundations of Distributed Consensus and Blockchains_](https://www.distributedconsensus.net/).

## Earlier teaching

From 2015 to 2020, I worked as a private tutor in mathematics and physics for pupils and university students, including preparation for the German Abitur.
